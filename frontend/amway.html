How do we craft the highest quality home for your Nutrilite All Plant Protein? 🌱

Each canister starts out as plastic pellets, which we blend and heat into molten form. We then shape it with cool molds and blown air to create a durable container. Once the canister then sets and strengthens on the cooling tracks, our technicians perform a series of quality checks to ensure no cracks, imperfections, or weaknesses to the canister. After a batch of canisters passes all of these checks, they head over to our Nutritional Products Plant to be filled with All Plant Protein.

Then it’s time for these products to leave our manufacturing facilities in Ada and begin their journey to your home!
